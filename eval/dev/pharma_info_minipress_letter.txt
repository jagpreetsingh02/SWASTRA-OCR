NEW MINIPRESS Capsules
(prazosin HCl)
1mg, 2mg, 5mg

COMPOSITION
Each capsule contains prazosin hydrochloride equivalent to prazosin 1 mg, 2 mg or 5 mg.

INDICATIONS
Hypertension. Benign prostatic hyperplasia.

DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
The initial dose is 0.5 mg at bedtime. Dosage may be increased gradually.

WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS
First-dose effect: postural hypotension may occur.

ADVERSE REACTIONS
Dizziness, headache, drowsiness.
